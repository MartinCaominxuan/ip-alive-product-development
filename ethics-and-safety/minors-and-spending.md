# Minors and Spending

## Purpose

Identify early safeguards and open questions related to children, teens, guardians, purchases, and loyalty systems.

## Project Status Note

This project is private, exploratory, and not affiliated with any named IP company. Any references to real companies should be treated only as hypothetical examples or future case-study candidates, not clients or partners.

## Guiding Questions

- How is age handled without over-collecting data?
- What guardian controls are required?
- Which spending mechanics should be limited or avoided?

## Assumptions and Hypotheses

- Assumption: Products involving beloved characters may create heightened spending-pressure risks for minors.
