# Limitations

## Product and engineering

- This is a local high-fidelity prototype, not a production service.
- There is no backend, cloud database, real authentication, multi-device sync, production payment system, or server-authoritative entitlement ledger.
- Offline redemption demonstrates behavior but cannot provide commercial anti-counterfeit or cross-device one-time guarantees.
- Conversation is scripted. AI safeguards have not been validated against production models, costs, latency, adversarial traffic, or provider changes.
- Long-term memory is persisted conversation and authored records, not semantic retrieval or verified autobiographical memory.
- Match-3, travel, wardrobe, shop, Life, and event systems have limited content depth and no production balancing.
- Local backups are integrity-checked but not encrypted.
- Production monitoring, incident response, abuse operations, and service objectives do not exist.

## Evidence and research

- Survey data have not yet been supplied or analyzed in this repository.
- Limited post-purchase blind-box engagement remains a hypothesis until results are cleaned and reported.
- No causal claim can currently be made that IP Alive reduces anxiety, improves well-being or retention, or extends attachment.
- No completed external usability study, longitudinal study, partner pilot, willingness-to-pay study, or commercial validation is documented.
- The honors dyadic study and IP Alive share an interest in stability and uncertainty, but examine different relationships and mechanisms. Evidence cannot automatically transfer between them.
- Personal experience is relevant reflexive context, not generalizable evidence.

## Disability and accessibility

Disability and accessibility needs have not yet been adequately solved. The prototype has not completed:

- screen-reader and semantic-label auditing;
- keyboard and switch-control navigation;
- focus-order and focus-restoration testing;
- dynamic type and text-reflow verification;
- reduced-motion alternatives;
- color-contrast and color-independence testing;
- captions, transcripts, and non-audio alternatives;
- cognitive-accessibility and plain-language review;
- testing with disabled participants.

This may exclude users and weaken usability findings. It must be disclosed in graduate applications, portfolio materials, demonstrations, acquisition discussions, and future study reports. Deferral does not remove the responsibility; it remains known product debt requiring an owner before public launch.

## Relationship and mental-health boundaries

- Perceived consistency from an AI character is not equivalent to reciprocal human attachment.
- The product must not claim AI relationships are superior to or should replace human relationships.
- Predictability can feel stabilizing while also creating dependency, avoidance, disappointment, or manipulation risks.
- Characters must not imply consciousness, exclusivity, abandonment distress, or punishment for disengagement.
- The product is not therapy, crisis care, diagnosis, or a substitute for professional or interpersonal support.
- Research involving trauma, loneliness, uncertainty, or emotional dependence requires careful consent and appropriate institutional review.

## IP, historical, and cultural limitations

- No existing-IP owner has approved this system.
- Licensed characters require authorization, canon materials, quotation rules, multilingual review, and a takedown process.
- Historical fiction must distinguish fact, inference, and fiction and avoid copying modern adaptations.
- Living, recently deceased, and hero/martyr figures require heightened legal and ethical review.
- International launch creates jurisdiction-specific privacy, consumer, AI, child-safety, health, payment, and IP duties.

## Commercial limitations

- There is no validated buyer, partner, price, revenue model, retention benchmark, or proven unit economics.
- Placeholder art limits evidence about visual appeal and collectible desirability.
- A buyer still needs substantial investment in infrastructure, art, content, law, accessibility, safety operations, and research.
- Completion percentages are planning estimates, not independent audits or valuation metrics.

## Research limitations

- The available consumer survey is a convenience sample of 165 responses, with most product-experience questions answered by 150 purchasers.
- The sample is disproportionately female and aged 18–22; recruitment method, response rate, and sampling frame are not documented in the aggregate export.
- Six respondents selected “under 18” despite consent text stating that continuation indicated age 18 or older. Eligibility treatment requires a documented decision using row-level data.
- Only aggregate tables are currently available. Individual-level records, complete open-text responses, missingness checks, reproducible statistical tests, and transparent qualitative coding are absent.
- Immediate post-unboxing connection was recalled retrospectively rather than measured at unboxing, so recall and recency effects may influence the observed difference.
- Stated interest and willingness to pay do not establish actual adoption, retention, revenue, or psychological benefit.
- The survey concerns consumers. It does not validate enterprise buyer demand, licensor approval, procurement feasibility, or partner willingness to acquire the product.
