# Product Vision

## Purpose

Define the long-term product direction for IP Alive without presenting unverified assumptions as facts.

## Project Status Note

This project is private, exploratory, and not affiliated with any named IP company. Any references to real companies should be treated only as hypothetical examples or future case-study candidates, not clients or partners.

## Guiding Questions

- What user and enterprise problems might the platform address?
- What would make the experience meaningfully character-driven rather than generic AI assistance?
- What must remain under official IP-holder control?

## Assumptions and Hypotheses

- Assumption: Companies with large fictional-character portfolios may seek new digital engagement models.
- Assumption: Collectors may value companions that connect physical ownership to daily-life utility.
