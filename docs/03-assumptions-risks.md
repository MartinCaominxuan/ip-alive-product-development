# Assumptions and Risks

## Purpose

Track unverified assumptions, hypotheses, and risks that require research, legal review, technical evaluation, or stakeholder feedback.

## Project Status Note

This project is private, exploratory, and not affiliated with any named IP company. Any references to real companies should be treated only as hypothetical examples or future case-study candidates, not clients or partners.

## Guiding Questions

- Which assumptions are most critical to validate first?
- What safety risks are unique to character-based AI companions?
- Which enterprise requirements could block adoption?

## Assumptions and Hypotheses

- Assumption: IP holders may require strict personality, voice, and content controls.
- Assumption: Users may expect emotional continuity across interactions.
- Preliminary evidence: official-character inconsistency was selected by 48.00% of purchasers as a reason for discomfort or reduced willingness to use, supporting character fidelity as a high-priority design requirement.
- Preliminary evidence: 39.33% described weaker connection than at unboxing, but 22.67% described stronger connection; connection decay is not universal.
- Unvalidated hypothesis: IP Alive can reduce or delay connection decay.
- Unvalidated hypothesis: an enterprise buyer will fund the infrastructure and governance required for a licensed deployment.

## Priority risks and decisions

- Acquire de-identified row-level data before running statistical tests or making subgroup claims.
- Resolve the six under-18 responses against the stated eligibility rule.
- Test whether users return to the working prototype rather than relying on stated interest.
- Decide whether activation defaults to unboxing or explicit opt-in; the survey supports both routes.
- Avoid assuming subscriptions: only 4.00% selected a low monthly subscription, while 65.33% preferred inclusion or a small one-time fee.
- Design continuity and export expectations because 33.33% were concerned that service shutdown could make the character disappear.
