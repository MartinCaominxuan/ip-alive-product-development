# IP Alive

## Product Proposition
IP Alive is an exploratory B2B platform concept that helps character-IP owners connect physical collectibles and digital character assets with persistent AI companions and everyday utility.

## Current Project Stage
IP Alive is in early product discovery, focused on framing the opportunity, defining assumptions, shaping research questions, and exploring responsible product directions before validation or implementation.

## Primary Research and Design Themes
- Character-IP licensing models and partner value propositions
- Physical-to-digital collectible experiences and ownership verification
- Persistent AI companion behavior, memory, identity, and continuity
- Everyday utility beyond novelty, including routines, reminders, education, and play
- Trust, safety, privacy, moderation, and age-appropriate interaction design
- B2B go-to-market assumptions, stakeholder incentives, and adoption risks
