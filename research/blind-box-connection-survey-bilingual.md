# IP Alive Blind-Box Connection Survey / IP Alive 盲盒连接感问卷

## Purpose / 研究目的

This survey is designed for people who have purchased blind boxes or similar character collectibles. It examines how emotional connection, attention, and interaction with a collectible change after purchase, including when and why the sense of connection begins to weaken.

本问卷面向购买过盲盒或类似角色收藏品的人群，旨在了解购买后用户对收藏品的情感连接、关注与互动如何随时间变化，以及连接感何时、为何开始减弱。

**Recommended survey length / 建议完成时间:** 5–7 minutes / 5–7 分钟  
**Recommended format / 建议形式:** Anonymous product-discovery survey / 匿名产品探索问卷

---

## Analysis Notes / 分析说明

- Report Q12 as an interval distribution rather than converting categories into exact days without justification. / 将Q12作为时间区间分布报告，不应在缺乏依据时强行换算为精确天数。
- Compare decay onset by initial satisfaction, prior IP familiarity, purchase frequency, and current interaction behavior. / 比较初始满意度、购买前IP熟悉度、购买频率和当前互动行为与连接衰减起点的关系。
- Separate respondents who never formed a connection from those whose initially strong connection later declined. / 将“从未形成连接”的用户与“初始连接较强、后来减弱”的用户分开分析。
- Avoid interpreting retrospective responses as exact longitudinal evidence. The results identify hypotheses for a later diary or longitudinal study. / 不要将回溯性回答解释为精确的纵向证据；结果应用于形成后续日记研究或纵向研究的假设。
- Pilot both language versions with native speakers before launch to check semantic equivalence and item clarity. / 正式投放前，应分别由中英文母语者预测试，以检查语义等价性和题目清晰度。
